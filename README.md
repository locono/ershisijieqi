# 二十四节气 · 中国人的时间哲学

> 以太阳周年视运动为尺，将一年划分为二十四个节气。

一个精美的二十四节气主题单页网站，采用中国传统宣纸风格设计，纯原生 HTML/CSS/JavaScript 实现，零依赖。

**中文** | [English](#en)

---

## ✨ 功能特性

### 📅 节气内容

- **二十四节气详情** — 每个节气的时令、物候、农事、风俗、养生与诗词
- **七十二候** — 五天为一候，每节气三候，完整呈现古人的物候观察
- **节气诗话** — 各节气相关的经典诗词赏析
- **十二时辰** — 十二时辰对照表，含时辰名、时间区间与别称
- **文化长卷** — 历法渊源、申遗历程、物候知识、农事指南

### 🎨 设计特色

- **宣纸质感** — 传统宣纸纹理 + 颗粒质感背景
- **四季配色** — 春绿、夏红、秋金、冬蓝，应季而变
- **书法字体** — 楷体/宋体为主，排版典雅
- **印章元素** — 朱红印章点缀，古意盎然
- **响应式设计** — 完美适配桌面、平板、手机

### ⚡ 交互体验

- **二十四节气盘** — 交互式圆形节气盘，悬停预览，点击进入详情
- **今日节气** — 自动推算当前节气、进度条、下一节气倒计时
- **四时运行时间线** — 春夏秋冬四季时间线，每季六节气
- **时令粒子动效** — 春落花、夏流光、秋落叶、冬飘雪，随季节变换
- **顶部导航栏** — 平滑滚动跳转，当前模块自动高亮
- **滚动阅读进度条** — 顶部实时显示阅读进度
- **返回顶部按钮** — 滚动后显示，一键回到顶部
- **滚动显现动画** — 内容区块随滚动渐入
- **深色模式友好** — 支持系统 `prefers-reduced-motion` 偏好

# Twenty-Four Solar Terms · The Chinese Philosophy of Time

> Measuring the sun's apparent annual journey, the year is divided into twenty-four solar terms.

A polished single-page website themed on the twenty-four solar terms, designed in the style of traditional Chinese rice paper and built with pure, dependency-free HTML/CSS/JavaScript.

[中文](#zh) | **English**

---

## ✨ Features

### 📅 Solar Term Content

- **Twenty-four solar terms in detail** — season cues, phenology, farm work, customs, health care, and poetry for every term
- **Seventy-two pentads** — five days make one pentad, three pentads per term, a complete record of ancient phenological observation
- **Poetry of the terms** — appreciation of classic poems associated with each term
- **Twelve double hours** — a reference table of the traditional two-hour periods, with their names, time ranges, and aliases
- **Cultural scroll** — origins of the calendar, the UNESCO inscription journey, phenology knowledge, and farming guidance

### 🎨 Design

- **Rice-paper texture** — traditional xuan paper grain over a finely textured background
- **Four-season palette** — spring green, summer red, autumn gold, winter blue, shifting with the season
- **Calligraphic typography** — Kai and Song typefaces as the base, elegantly composed
- **Seal motifs** — vermilion seal stamps as accents, rich in antique charm
- **Responsive layout** — fits desktop, tablet, and phone

### ⚡ Interaction

- **Solar term wheel** — an interactive circular dial; hover to preview, click for details
- **Today's term** — automatic calculation of the current term, with a progress bar and a countdown to the next
- **Timeline of the four seasons** — spring, summer, autumn, and winter, six terms per season
- **Seasonal particle effects** — spring blossoms, summer light, autumn leaves, winter snow, changing with the season
- **Sticky top navigation** — smooth scrolling with automatic highlighting of the current section
- **Reading progress bar** — live progress shown at the top of the page
- **Back to top** — appears after scrolling, one click to return
- **Scroll reveal** — content blocks fade in as you scroll
- **Dark-mode friendly** — respects the system `prefers-reduced-motion` preference
